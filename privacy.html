Battery Emoji — Privacy information

Publisher: Zoopet Apps. Updated 5 October 2026. Android package: app.lumapippa.batterypets.

Battery Emoji personalizes your Android device. This notice explains the information the app processes and the controls available to you. For questions, contact contact@zoopet.in.

Local personalization

Your chosen companion, colours, size, position, display mode, saved looks, favourites, quiet hours and motion preferences are stored on your device. Android supplies the current battery level, charging state and power-saving state. The app uses these readings to display the companion and battery widget. The personalization features do not read the content of other apps, capture screens, listen to the microphone, access contacts or request device location. Advertising providers may infer an approximate location from an IP address, as described below. Display-over-other-apps permission draws the companion window and, when selected, one non-interactive decoration strip. Shapes and indicators do not replace Android system icons or alter device settings.

Screen playground and local status indicators

Movement style, notch dimensions, icon styling and up to five sticker choices are stored locally with appearance settings and may be included in a manually shared setup code. Selected status icons use read-only Android network-state and Wi-Fi-state access and available cellular signal callbacks. The app does not request location or phone-state runtime permission, scan networks, read SSIDs, BSSIDs, phone numbers or device identifiers, or send these indicator readings to the publisher. Indicators show unknown when a reading is unavailable; mobile data refers to the active default cellular connection, not a device-setting switch. Normal advertising/provider data flows remain separately described below.

A battery demo uses a clearly marked temporary percentage inside Battery Emoji only. It never changes real Android battery information or widget readings, is not stored in saved looks or setup codes, and expires after at most five minutes. Screen-off, Pause and Stop clear it. Starting a demo does not start the overlay service.

Control Starting the floating companion is optional. You can pause or stop it in the app or its ongoing notification, and you can revoke display permission in Android Settings. The companion is hidden when the screen is off or locked. The home-screen widget can be refreshed manually when the floating service is stopped. Android and device manufacturers may limit background operation.

Advertising

Versions distributed through app stores may include Google Mobile Ads, Google's User Messaging Platform, and mediation adapters for Unity Ads, Meta Audience Network, Pangle, AppLovin, Mintegral, Liftoff Monetize (Vungle) and InMobi. Versions without commerce do not request ads or purchases. When advertising is enabled, these advertising services may collect and share the device's IP address and approximate location inferred from it, device or account-related identifiers, ad or app interactions, and diagnostic information for advertising, analytics, functionality and fraud prevention. Unity's disclosure also covers purchase history and, where enabled in its dashboard, app-usage timing. Other mediation partners have their own disclosures and configuration choices, which the publisher must review before release. These advertising services can process data even when personalized advertising is restricted. The exact processing depends on the enabled SDKs, privacy choices and provider configuration. The app does not send its cosmetic setup codes or store purchase tokens as ad-targeting fields. Advertising consent is requested through the provider's consent flow before ad initialization. Privacy options, where required, are available in Settings. Ads are shown only within the app and never in the floating companion over another app.

Purchases

Optional cosmetic packs and Remove Ads use the store where the app was installed. Payment details are handled by that store. The app processes product identifiers, purchase status and purchase tokens or receipt identifiers. When the configured verification service is enabled, it receives the store receipt and an app-installation identifier to verify ownership and issue a time-limited signed entitlement. Failed verification requests may be stored in an encrypted local retry queue. The verification service uses transaction data to check the current purchase state and prevent invalid grants. Your receipt is not included in a shared setup code. Purchases, restoration and periodic ownership checks require internet. A successful ownership check allows paid features to work offline for up to 24 hours; once this period expires, reconnect to verify ownership and restore access. This time limit also applies to Remove Ads. A refund or revocation can remove a paid entitlement after revalidation or when the offline period expires. A failed network check does not itself cancel a store purchase.

Sharing

Sharing a setup code opens Android's share sheet. The code contains cosmetic setup choices, not a purchase receipt, account identifier or entitlement. A recipient still needs ownership of any paid cosmetic they choose to apply. The app does not automatically publish your setup or contact anyone.

Analytics and accounts

The app does not require registration and does not use Firebase analytics. Core features work offline without an account. Advertising and purchase providers operate their own network services where those features are available. An optional ad-control check can retrieve display settings from the publisher without sending a setup code, advertising ID or receipt; the server still receives ordinary network connection information such as an IP address. There is no automatic marketing notification or social posting system.

Deletion and retention

Use Clear local data to reset local personalization and stop the companion. Uninstalling removes app-local data, subject to Android's storage behavior; app backup is disabled in the manifest. Clearing data does not cancel a store purchase or erase the store's transaction records. Contact the distributing store about its transaction history, and contact the app publisher about records held by the configured verification service. Do not email passwords, complete payment details or purchase tokens.

Audience and changes

Battery Emoji is designed for adult personalization. Changes to the app or its services may require an updated notice. The current notice is available in Settings and at the public policy address provided by the distributing store.
